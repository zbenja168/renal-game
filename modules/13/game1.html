🎉 Mechanism mapped!

Thiazides block NCC (Na+/Cl- symporter) in the DCT. Less Na+ inside the cell → NCX exports more Ca2+ → less Ca2+ in urine. Volume depletion → RAAS → PT Ca2+ reabsorption increases. Thiazides RETAIN calcium (opposite of loops) — hence they prevent kidney stones and may help bone density.
