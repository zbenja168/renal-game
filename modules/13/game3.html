Match each physiologic effect to the diuretic class that causes it

Goal: Thiazides and loop diuretics overlap in some effects but differ critically in others — especially calcium handling and potency. Flip cards to find matching pairs. Six pairs total.

🎉 Diuretic contrast mastered!

Key difference: loops WASTE calcium (lose lumen-positive charge in TAL), while thiazides RETAIN calcium (increase DCT Ca2+ reabsorption via NCX). Both cause hypokalemia + metabolic alkalosis, but loops are more potent. Thiazides can cause hyponatremia; loops can cause hyponatremia only in SIADH setting.
