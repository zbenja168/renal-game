Calcium stones (75%) = most common; hypercalciuria, hyperoxaluria, hypocitraturia. Struvite = infection stones from urease-positive bacteria; alkaline urine; staghorn calculi. Uric acid = acidic urine (pH <5.5), gout, high purine diet; radiolucent. Cystine = rare genetic cystinuria, hexagonal crystals, recurrent stones in children.
