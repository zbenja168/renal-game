Crystal morphology on urinalysis gives a rapid clue to stone type: dumbbell/envelope back = calcium oxalate; coffin-lid rectangles = struvite (infection); hexagonal = cystine. Uric acid crystals are rhomboid or rosette-shaped. These shapes guide both diagnosis and initial treatment choices.
