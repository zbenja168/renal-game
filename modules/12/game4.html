Adverse effects and interactions

🎉 Adverse effects sorted!

Electrolyte imbalances dominate: hypokalemia + metabolic alkalosis (contraction alkalosis) is the classic combination. Volume depletion can cause prerenal AKI and hypotension. Ototoxicity is the unique non-electrolyte toxicity — worst with ethacrynic acid and rapid IV dosing.
