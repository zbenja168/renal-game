Goal: Flip cards to find matching pairs. Each physiological or pathological change is paired with the direction of its effect on the glomerular filtration rate, as described in the brick.

Anything that raises P_GC (afferent dilation, efferent constriction) increases GFR. Anything that raises P_BS (obstruction) or raises π_GC (concentrated plasma proteins) decreases GFR. Hypoalbuminemia lowers π_GC and initially raises GFR.
