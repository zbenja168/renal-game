Match each component of the ADH axis to its role

Goal: The osmoreceptor-ADH-collecting duct axis is how the body senses and corrects serum osmolality. Match each component or trigger to its correct role. Six pairs total.
Pairs found: 0 / 6 Flips: 0

🎉 ADH axis matched!

Osmoreceptors in the hypothalamus sense rising osmolality → trigger ADH release from posterior pituitary → ADH acts on vasopressin receptors in the collecting duct → AQP2 channels are inserted → water is reabsorbed → urine is concentrated. Without ADH, urine osmolality can fall as low as 50 mOsm/kg.
