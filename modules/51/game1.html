More than 95% of AIN is caused by infection or allergic drug reactions. NSAIDs are the most common overall cause. PPIs are increasingly recognized. Antibiotics (penicillin, cephalosporins, rifampin) cause classic allergic AIN. Systemic diseases like SLE and Sjögren syndrome cause non-allergic AIN.
