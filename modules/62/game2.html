The key imaging clue for AML is fat on CT (dark density). Oncocytoma can mimic RCC almost perfectly — definitive diagnosis requires surgical pathology. RCC lacks fat but may enhance on CT with contrast. When fat is absent, resection is done to exclude RCC.
