Key facts

🎉 Mechanism complete!

Vaptans block V2 receptors on renal collecting duct cells, preventing aquaporin-2 insertion. Without aquaporins, the collecting duct is impermeable to water — dilute urine is excreted (aquaresis), raising serum sodium. This is the mechanism that corrects SIADH.
