Factors affecting distal K+ secretion

🎉 Distal secretion sorted!

High aldosterone, high plasma K+, high Na+ delivery, high urine flow, and alkalemia all increase K+ secretion. Low aldosterone (K+-sparing diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs), acidemia, and K+-sparing agents all decrease K+ secretion.
