~70% of filtered K+ is reabsorbed in the PT. Another ~20% is reabsorbed in the TAL via NKCC2. The cortical collecting duct is the KEY secretory site — principal cells secrete K+ via ROMK and BK channels under aldosterone control. This is where K+ balance is regulated.
