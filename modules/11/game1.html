🎉 K+ shifts mastered!

Insulin and beta-agonists drive K+ INTO cells (cause hypokalemia). Acidemia, hyperosmolality, exercise, and alpha-agonists shift K+ OUT OF cells (cause hyperkalemia). This internal balance is separate from total-body K+ balance.
