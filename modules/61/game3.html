🎉 Paraneoplastic syndromes matched!

RCC is notorious for paraneoplastic syndromes: ectopic EPO → polycythemia; PTHrP → hypercalcemia; ectopic renin → hypertension; ACTH → Cushing. Liver dysfunction from a non-hepatic primary tumor is Stauffer syndrome. Resolution of paraneoplastic findings after resection supports successful treatment.
