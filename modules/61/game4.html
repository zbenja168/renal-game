Treatment options

🎉 Treatment map complete!

Localized RCC: surgical excision is the cornerstone; partial (nephron-sparing) or radical nephrectomy depending on size. Metastatic RCC: cytoreductive nephrectomy ± immunotherapy (checkpoint inhibitors like ipilimumab + nivolumab); anti-angiogenic targeted agents (VEGF/mTOR pathway). RCC is notoriously resistant to standard chemotherapy and radiation.
