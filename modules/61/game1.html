Clinical features

🎉 RCC presentation mastered!

The classic triad is hematuria + flank pain + palpable mass — but it is only present in ~10% of patients. Most RCC is discovered incidentally. Paraneoplastic syndromes (EPO → polycythemia; PTHrP → hypercalcemia; renin → hypertension; ACTH → Cushing) can precede the diagnosis. The biggest risk factor is tobacco smoking.
