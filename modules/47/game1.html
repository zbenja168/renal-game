Anti-GBM disease is a pulmonary-renal syndrome. Antibodies attack type IV collagen in glomerular and alveolar basement membranes. Lung damage causes hemoptysis and dyspnea; kidney damage causes hematuria and rapidly declining GFR. Goodpasture syndrome classically refers to the combined renal and pulmonary presentation.
