Steps to sequence

🎉 Sequence complete!

Anti-GBM disease begins with autoantibody formation against alpha-3 type IV collagen in the GBM and alveolar BM. Antibody binding activates a type II cytotoxic immune response, recruiting inflammatory cells that destroy both the glomerulus and lung alveoli. Without plasmapheresis and immunosuppression, the disease progresses rapidly to renal failure.
