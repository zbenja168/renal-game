Magnesium facts

🎉 Mg²⁺ pathway complete!

Magnesium is absorbed in the ileum via paracellular solvent drag and TRPM6/7 channels. The thick ascending limb reabsorbs ~70% of filtered Mg²⁺ paracellularly, driven by the positive lumen potential from NKCC2. The DCT fine-tunes Mg²⁺ via active TRPM6-dependent transcellular transport.
