🎉 Osmoles classified!

Effective osmoles (sodium, glucose, mannitol, albumin) cannot freely cross cell membranes and drive water movement. Ineffective osmoles (urea, ethanol, methanol) cross freely and do not cause water shifts — so they raise osmolality without affecting tonicity or cell volume.
