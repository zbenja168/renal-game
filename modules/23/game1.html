Causes of hyperkalemia

Most hyperkalemia results from reduced renal K+ excretion (CKD, spironolactone, ACE inhibitors). Cell breakdown and diabetic ketoacidosis shift K+ out of cells. Hemolysis can falsely elevate the lab value — always check for pseudohyperkalemia.
