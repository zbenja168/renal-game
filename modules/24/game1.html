The proximal tubule handles most calcium (paracellular) and nearly all phosphate reabsorption (Na-phosphate cotransporter). The thick ascending limb reabsorbs calcium paracellularly via the calcium sensor. The DCT reabsorbs calcium transcellularly under PTH and calcitriol control — the fine-tuning segment.
