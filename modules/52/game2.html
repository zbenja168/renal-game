The renal papilla is the most vulnerable zone: it is the innermost, most concentrated, and most hyperosmolar region, supplied only by the vasa recta — small arteries that contain high-oncotic-pressure plasma after glomerular filtration. Any reduction in flow here leads to ischemia and necrosis.
