🎉 Creatinine assessed!

Creatinine is endogenous, inexpensive, and practical — but tubular secretion inflates clearance by 10–20%, it's muscle-mass dependent, and serum levels lag 24–48 h behind acute GFR changes. That is why inulin, cystatin C, or measured clearance is sometimes preferred.
