🎉 All four cases cracked!

True hematuria = RBCs on microscopy. Myoglobinuria = heme+ dipstick with no RBCs (rhabdomyolysis). Drug/food pigments = heme-negative, no RBCs. Hemoglobinuria = heme+ with no RBCs (hemolysis). The dipstick + microscopy combination is the key to telling them apart.
