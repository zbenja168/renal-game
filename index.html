Renal Bricks

Interactive games to reinforce the renal system curriculum, one brick at a time.

Week 1 · Foundations

Anatomy · Physiology · Diuretics · Sodium & water balance

21 bricks. Each module has 6 short games covering its learning objectives, capped by a comprehensive boss round.

Week 2 · Clinical Applications

Electrolyte disorders · Acid-base · AKI/CKD/ESRD · Labs & imaging

18 bricks. Builds on week 1 with disease states, diagnostics, and management.

Week 3 · Glomerular & Tubulointerstitial Disease

Nephrotic & nephritic spectrum · ATN/AIN · UTIs · Stones · Schistosomiasis

16 bricks. Glomerular diseases, tubulo-interstitial injury, and lower urinary tract pathology.

Week 4 · Lower Urinary Tract & Renal Neoplasia

Histology · Congenital anomalies · Cystic kidney disease · Renal & bladder tumors · Micturition disorders

10 bricks. Lower urinary tract structure and function, cystic kidney disease, renal/bladder neoplasia, and bladder dysfunction.

Built from the Brick Exchange source material. Educational use.
